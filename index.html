Study Tips & Techniques

Common study approaches to test against your course, schedule and learning needs.

01
Pomodoro Technique
Try a 25-minute work period followed by a 5-minute break, then adjust the interval if a different rhythm works better for the task. Pomofocus.io provides a simple timer.
02
Active Recall with Anki
Turn material into questions and test what you can retrieve without looking. Anki can schedule repeated review.
03
The Feynman Technique
Explain a concept in plain language, then check the explanation against your course material. Mark any step you could not explain precisely for review.
04
Practice with Past Exams
Use instructor-provided practice material and authorized past exams to rehearse the format and timing. CEMC publishes mathematics and computing contest material.
05
Interleaving Subjects
Mix a small set of related problem types during review, then check whether you can identify which method applies before solving each one.
06
Sleep Is Non-Negotiable
Protect a regular sleep schedule during busy weeks. If sleep problems persist or affect daily life, use a qualified health resource rather than relying on study advice.
