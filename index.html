Study Tips & Techniques

Evidence-backed strategies that top students swear by — plus tools to implement them.

01
Pomodoro Technique
Study for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break. After 4 rounds, take a 20-minute rest. Prevents burnout and maintains focus. Try Pomofocus.io to get started.
02
Active Recall with Anki
Don't just re-read notes — test yourself. Anki uses spaced repetition to help you memorize information permanently, not just for the night before.
03
The Feynman Technique
Explain a concept as if teaching it to someone else. If you can't, you don't understand it yet. This exposes gaps faster than re-reading ever could.
04
Practice with Past Exams
UW professors often recycle question styles. Use CEMC and department pages to find past papers and time yourself under exam conditions.
05
Interleaving Subjects
Switch between subjects rather than blocking one for an entire session. It feels harder but research shows you retain more and perform better on tests.
06
Sleep Is Non-Negotiable
Memory consolidation happens during sleep. Pulling all-nighters before exams actively harms performance. Aim for 7–8 hours — especially in finals season.
