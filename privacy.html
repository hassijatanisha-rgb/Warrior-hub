Privacy

Warrior Hub is a static directory. It does not ask for personal information or run its own analytics, advertising pixels, accounts or forms.

Information handled by this site

The site itself does not store form submissions, set first-party cookies or maintain a user database. GitHub Pages may process routine request information to deliver and protect the site under GitHub's own privacy terms.

Links to other services

Resource links open third-party websites. Those services may collect information or set cookies under their own policies. Warrior Hub does not control their content, availability or data practices.

Corrections and privacy questions

Use the public GitHub issue form. Do not include private, medical, academic or account information because submitted issues are publicly visible.
